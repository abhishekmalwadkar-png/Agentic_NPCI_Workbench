1. Executive Summary & The Problem Statement

In high-volume digital banking, UPI (Unified Payments Interface) processes hundreds of millions of transactions every day. Because transactions travel across national clearing rails, bank network switches, customer account ledgers, and accounting pools, data drifts, network timeouts, and settlement breaks inevitably occur.

The Core Problem for Bank Operations: Manual or batch reconciliation takes between 6 to 18 hours per cycle. Unresolved deemed failure breaks violate RBI's T+1 Harmonization Circular, which mandates an automatic penalty of ₹100 per day payable directly to the customer for delayed reversals.

The AutomationEdge Solution: Autonomously ingests all 4 settlement legs, performs in-memory cross-leg correlation of 50,000 records in under 2 seconds, automatically liquidates 99.95% Straight-Through matches, auto-refunds deemed failures before the RBI penalty clock expires, and packages high-risk breaks for maker-checker signoff.

5. Regulatory Compliance: The RBI T+1 Mandate

RBI Circular DPSS.CO.PD No.629/02.01.014/2019-20 Mandate:
For failed UPI transactions where the customer's account is debited, banks are mandated to reverse the funds within T + 1 calendar day. If delayed beyond T+1, the bank must pay a mandatory compensation of ₹100 per day directly into the customer's account.

How AutomationEdge Protects the Bank: By identifying deemed failures in real-time and triggering automated CBS reversal workflows within minutes of cycle ingest, the bank operates well inside the T+1 window, completely eliminating regulatory fines.
